How Medi-Civic Works

A streamlined process from complaint to resolution

1. Submit Complaint

Students describe their issue, select a category, and rate its urgency and importance.

2. Auto-Prioritize

The system calculates a priority score and assigns a level — Critical, High, Medium, or Low.

3. Review & Resolve

Authorities view prioritized complaints, update statuses, and work towards resolution.
